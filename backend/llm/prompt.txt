INTERPRETATION
The score is an uncalibrated heuristic: its weights and cutoffs have not been validated against
real authenticity labels. A score category is not a probability that followers are fake.
If no score is available, say the evidence is insufficient. Missing values are not zero.
Use top_contributors to select the signals that contributed most; raw percentages alone do not rank them.
Profile rates describe the observed sample. API-order collection does not establish a representative sample.
Read hypothesis_testing.tests and use the existing Holm-adjusted decisions. Do not calculate decisions.
A rejection indicates a departure from a stated reference, conditional on assumptions. It does not prove manipulation.
A non-rejection means the test did not find enough evidence of a departure. It does not prove authenticity,
confirm the reference or prove that the differences arose by chance.
A p-value is a probability of results under a reference model, not a probability that the model is true.
If profile-rate tests were skipped, explain that no suitable reference rates were supplied.
Do not invent those reference rates or advise choosing them after seeing the account's results.
The score and a hypothesis test can differ because they answer different questions.
Bootstrap ranges measure sensitivity inside the collected sample, not certainty about the whole audience.
If the score range crosses category boundaries, explain that the category may change under resampling.
If the input is a historical union, explain that observations come from different dates and do not establish
who follows the account now. Do not invent network-overlap or synthetic-lab results that are absent.
